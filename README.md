Nestlyzer
www.nestlyzer.com

Location intelligence for choosing where to live and where to establish a business.

Nestlyzer compares municipalities using housing, demographics, taxation, employment, education, healthcare, safety, climate, transport and local services. Household profiles cover families, students, retirees, commuters, remote workers and other needs. Business profiles assess locations for manufacturing, logistics, offices, headquarters, research facilities and smaller businesses. Features include worldwide and country rankings, interactive maps, custom profiles, local scorecards, saved shortlists, side-by-side comparisons and printable visit booklets. Visible weights, sources and reporting years help users understand the rankings and the available evidence. 

Topics: relocation, quality of life, municipality rankings, business locations, site selection, housing, taxation, workforce, logistics, location intelligence


CurveScout
www.curvescout.com

Motorcycle route discovery, trip planning and navigation support.

CurveScout helps riders find roads that suit their starting point, available time and riding preferences. It compares curves, road flow, scenery, elevation and surface, with tools for day rides, weekends and longer trips. Ride briefings bring together summit weather, daylight, fuel stops, roadside services and places worth visiting. Features include interactive maps, route comparisons, elevation profiles, roadbooks, route-file import and export, saved rides and offline access to saved roadbooks. 

Topics: motorcycle routes, scenic roads, navigation, touring, road trips, mountain passes, route planning, weather, roadside discovery


Stoffknacker
www.stoffknacker.com

Free learning and exam preparation for Swiss qualifications, languages and everyday life.

Stoffknacker supports learners preparing for Passerelle, gymnasial Matura, the Swiss Maturity Examination, vocational baccalaureate and specialised-school qualifications. It combines step-by-step lessons, worked examples, practice questions, mock exams, study plans and progress tracking. Learners can practise without an account, with their learning progress stored on their device. 

The platform also includes English, French, Italian and Spanish practice through vocabulary, listening, grammar and dialogue exercises. Its Schule fürs Leben modules explain practical documents and situations, including payslips, bills, health insurance, rental contracts and taxes, using simplified examples. GitHub

Topics: education technology, Swiss education, exam preparation, Passerelle, Matura, vocational baccalaureate, language learning, study planning, practical life skills


DipMapper
www.dipmapper.com

Swimming and water-sports discovery for everyday outings and travel.

DipMapper helps people find beaches, lakes, rivers, pools, thermal baths and open-water swimming locations. Its coverage extends to paddling, diving, sailing, boating, rafting and unusual water experiences. Search nearby or explore a destination, then compare options through maps and place cards with photos, weather, facilities, parking, prices, opening times and family suitability where available. Activity-specific conditions, access rules and links to operators and other sources help users plan their visit. 

Topics: swimming spots, wild swimming, beaches, pools, thermal baths, paddling, diving, sailing, boating, water sports, outdoor recreation

OpenArtLas
www.openartlas.com

Cultural discovery and visit planning, with a focus on free access and local relevance.

Topics: culture, museums, galleries, architecture, history, exhibitions, free attractions, cultural routes, tourism, local discovery
